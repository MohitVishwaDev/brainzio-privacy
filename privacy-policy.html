Your Privacy Is Respected

Brainzio is designed for safe, relaxing offline play. We do not require account registration, user logins, cloud profiles, or access to your contacts, camera, or personal files.

1. Information We Process

Brainzio is a lightweight multi-puzzle casual arcade game. We handle minimal, non-identifying technical data:

  • Local Game Progress & State: Your high scores, completed levels across all 5 puzzle games (Liquid Sort, Block Puzzle, Tile Match, 2048 Merge, and Number Connect), earned coins, unlocked fluid themes, and daily challenge records are stored strictly on your local device.
  • Device Diagnostics & Crash Performance: Standard non-identifying diagnostics (such as app freeze logs or memory consumption) may be recorded locally to maintain high 60fps performance and stability.
  • No Personal Profiling: We do not collect or request your real name, email address, physical location, phone contacts, photos, or biometric data.

  • Consent Management: Players can manage or reset consent choices at any time directly through device settings or through the in-game Privacy Settings option.
  • Third-Party Policy References: For further information regarding how Google processes advertising data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Guide.

3. Children & Family Safety (COPPA & Families Policy)

Brainzio is a general-audience casual brain puzzle game suitable for all ages. We strictly uphold Google Play's Families Policy and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under applicable local legal age).
  • All game puzzle mechanics run entirely client-side on the device without chat rooms, user messaging, or external player interactions.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has inadvertently provided any data, please reach out to us and we will promptly investigate and clear the relevant record.

4. Your Rights & Choices

  • Reset or Delete Game Data: Because your progress is stored locally, you have total control over your data. You may permanently erase all saved progress, coin balances, and scores at any time by clearing the app data in your device's Settings > Apps > Brainzio > Clear Storage or by uninstalling the application.
  • Opt Out of Interest-Based Advertising: You can opt out of personalized ad tracking globally on your Android device by visiting Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
